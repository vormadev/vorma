## Quick Read

- `kit/mux` hot-path optimization recovered the major loader regressions.
- Loader JSON allocs are now better than old baseline (`88` vs `91`), with a
  remaining CPU gap.
- Loader HTML now beats old baseline and alloc parity is restored.
- Cold-cache JSON allocs/bytes are better than old baseline, but CPU remains
  slower.
- Route deps/CSS and SSR are still slightly slower in CPU time.
